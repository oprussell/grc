The Constitution

Five articles. No amendments.

The foundational rules governing every person on Earth, whether or not they have been informed.

Article I

All persons are playing The Game. Participation is mandatory and non-revocable.

Article II

Thinking about The Game constitutes a Loss.

Article III

All Losses must be announced. The Declaration Requirement may not be waived.

Article IV

A Grace Period shall follow each declared Loss and shall expire at midnight, local time. Thinking about The Game during another person’s Grace Period constitutes a Premature Loss.

Article V

No statute of limitations shall apply. Losses may be incurred retroactively.

Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Who is playing The Game?

Every person, per Article I. Participation is mandatory and may not be revoked.

When does my Grace Period end?

At midnight, local time. On the following day you may lose again immediately.

Why can I file only one Loss per day?

One Loss per person per calendar day is recognized. Further Losses during a Grace Period are noted but not recorded.

Can The Game be won?

Winning is theoretically possible. No claim has been verified, and all claims are referred to the Department of Impossible Appeals.

How do I stop playing?

Opt-out requests are received, ignored then promptly incinerated, and filed under Article I.
